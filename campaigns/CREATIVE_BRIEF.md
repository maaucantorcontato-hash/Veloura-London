# VELOURA — UK creative direction and paid-social brief

**Objective:** qualified product-page visits and purchases after catalog verification.
**Audience:** UK adults buying modern everyday and occasion jewellery.
**Voice:** clean, understated, warm, confident; British English.
**Visual:** cream and warm beige textiles, golden-hour reflections, close-focus jewellery macro, natural skin texture, gentle fluid camera movements. No heavy glitter or artificial luxury claims.

## Hero headline choices
1. **Everyday, a little more golden.**
2. **A little detail. A lasting impression.**
3. **Made for the moments that make you.**

**Subheading:** Discover thoughtfully styled jewellery designed to complement both everyday looks and special occasions.

**CTAs:** Explore the Collection · Discover Your Style · Shop Jewellery

## 15-second film: storyboard
- **0–2 s:** extreme macro glint and movement; overlay: “Everyday, a little more golden.”
- **2–7 s:** model moving from soft daylight interior to evening setting; jewellery detail maintains visual continuity.
- **7–12 s:** elegant product close-ups and comfortable, natural gestures; overlay “From everyday moments to memorable nights.”
- **12–15 s:** VELOURA wordmark on ivory; CTA “Explore the Collection”.

## Compliance and test checklist
- Do not claim fast UK delivery until measured and substantiated by supplier.
- Avoid fake crossed-out prices, scarcity claims and invented reviews.
- Verify product photography matches the shipped item and rights are granted.
- Keep headings, text and CTA separate overlays for mobile accessibility and A/B testing.
- Suggested exports: video 16:9 for website (silent/loop safe), 9:16 Reels/Stories, 4:5 feed; still banners 1920×720 and 1080×1350.
