# VELOURA — Brand and Creative Assets

This repository now contains the **current VELOURA jewellery brand kit and creative production materials** for the UK market. It is **not** the live Wix Studio store and is **not** a product checkout.

## Source of truth

- Website: Wix Studio + Wix Stores (edited separately)
- Brand: VELOURA (not the older 'Velour London' fashion concept)
- Market: United Kingdom; English (UK); GBP (£)
- Style: ivory #F8F5EF, brown #35271F, warm gold #AE874A
- Primary mark: VELOURA wordmark. Secondary mark: gold V monogram.

## Files

- `assets/brand/` — SVG vector wordmarks, monograms, favicon and social profile artwork.
- `assets/creative-concepts/` — labelled **concept-only** banner and social ad compositions (not product photos).
- `campaigns/` — UK copy, hooks and 3 Higgsfield scene prompts.
- `products/` — product photography verification and sourcing tracker.
- `wix/` — steps to install safely without replacing working Wix controls.
- `index.html` — internal, noindex visual brand-kit preview; **not a shop**.

## Do not publish unverified claims

The eight Figma product names are design references, **not verified supplier SKUs**. Obtain product links, confirmed variants, authorised true-to-product photos, material specs, shipping/returns conditions, and final prices before publishing product listings or paid ads. Never imply that an AI concept image depicts the actual item shipped.

## Recovery

Older HTML, CSS and JS site files were removed from `main` after backup to branch `backup/legacy-velour-london-2026-11-09`. The previous Git commit remains recoverable. The active Wix site was not edited.

## Using in Wix

Download/upload SVG where Wix accepts it; otherwise export PNG from the vectors or use the separate VELOURA PNG/ICO kit supplied in the ChatGPT conversation. Keep the native Wix hamburger and cart controls functional. See `wix/INSTALLATION.md`.

## Retail pricing
VELOURA's working policy is to price slightly below comparable UK jewellery retailers where margins allow. Verify current competitor retail prices and landed fulfilment costs before any Wix catalog price is approved. See [products/PRICING_POLICY_UK.md](products/PRICING_POLICY_UK.md).
