# VELOURA — UK Retail Pricing Policy (Working Standard)

## Owner's strategic instruction (2026-11-10)
**Retail prices should be slightly below comparable competitor shops in the United Kingdom**, regardless of product category, **provided the product remains commercially viable**. Competitor pricing is a strategic target, not permission to sell at a loss or to copy misleading comparisons.

## Practical target
- Aim **approximately 5–10% below a well-grounded benchmark of comparable UK retail offers** as an initial guideline, not a mandatory percentage. The brand owner specified "slightly below"; 5–10% is a proposed working interpretation, subject to real market evidence.
- Compare **the same or truly comparable item**: material, plating, dimensions, design, packaging, warranty/returns conditions and shipping service.
- Use several UK-targeting stores, ideally **at least 3 verified comparable retail offers**, with source links and observation dates. A single Amazon, AliExpress, marketplace promo, or influencer listing is not a reliable retail benchmark by itself.
- Compare the **consumer's delivered price** (item, shipping and mandatory taxes where relevant). Confirm current promotion and available sizes/colours.
- Use the median of the comparable delivered prices as a starting reference; if a well-matched leading competitor is the main alternative, capture both its price and the median.
- Do **not** invent competitor quotes, rankings, sales volume or supplier costs. Without fresh evidence, leave the proposed VELOURA price pending.

## Profitability gate before publication
For every selected real SKU/variant, calculate:

**Expected per-order contribution = VELOURA retail receipts − sourcing cost for that variant − supplier shipping − expected taxes/duties borne by the merchant − payment/platform fees − expected returns/refunds − expected advertising acquisition cost − other variable fulfilment charges.**

State tax basis explicitly so VAT is not double-counted. Consider fixed operating expenses separately when judging whether a price is sustainable. Do not confuse markup with margin.

If the target price below competitors leaves an unacceptable contribution or cannot meet customer expectations:
1. Negotiate a better repeat-order supplier rate or consolidated shipping.
2. Choose a different variant, supplier or product.
3. Offer a genuinely different service/value proposition.
4. Do not publish with a loss-making price simply to undercut.

## Price decision worksheet
For each approved product, record:
- SKU and supplier listing URL, selected variant and actual **repeat-order** quote.
- Total landed cost to UK and whether price includes VAT.
- Three or more comparable competitor item/variant URLs, observed delivered price and date.
- UK competitor benchmark, proposed VELOURA price (GBP), approximate % difference, gross margin and contribution after costs.
- Explicit owner approval before publishing a live price.
- Review cadence: before launch, then periodically as sourcing/competitor prices change.

## Rules for honest advertising
- Avoid fake crossed-out prices or invented "was £x" reference prices.
- Don't claim a competitor saving without a grounded like-for-like comparison.
- Promotional AliExpress new-customer or gift prices cannot automatically serve as the repeat-order cost basis.
- Maintain consistency between product card, product page and checkout prices.

This is a working commercial policy, not evidence that any competitor's current price has been checked. The Wix store remains unchanged until specific products and prices are approved.
