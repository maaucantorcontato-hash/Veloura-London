## Fast no-cost store comparison workflow

For each shop:
1. Set the AliExpress destination to United Kingdom and currency to GBP.
2. If available, select **Products > Earrings**, then order results by **Orders / Best Selling**; do not mistake sponsored results for winners.
3. Open **one exact jewellery product** rather than a supplies/DIY component bundle. Capture product ID and canonical `/item/...html` URL, seller display name, the selected **gold variant** and complete item price.
4. Look for rating and count of reviews (not stars alone), plus recent **buyer photographs**. Favor consistent recent quality evidence rather than only big historical order numbers.
5. Check the advertised UK shipping and VAT basis, realistic delivery estimates, and **repeat-order supplier price**. New-customer, 'free with purchase' and coupon prices are not repeatable procurement costs.
6. Verify product materials/claims, packaging, commercial reuse rights for photos and whether the seller supports dropshipping/neutral packaging. Consider ordering a sample before paid promotion.
7. Compare to **3 comparable UK retail prices** and target VELOURA modestly below where contribution margin stays positive. See `PRICING_POLICY_UK.md`.

## Shortlisting rubric (0–5 each)
- Style and brand fit
- Product quality evidence: real buyer photos and verified material
- UK shipping predictability and total cost
- Sustainable margin versus competing UK shops
- Dependability of the seller: responsiveness, reviews, repeat-order price and rights

Do not classify a store as approved based only on a link or on 'Orders' sorting. The initial target is a small collection of 8 verified sellable items across multiple sources.
