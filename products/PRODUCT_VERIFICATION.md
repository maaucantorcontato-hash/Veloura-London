# Product assets — approval workflow

The eight names in `product-status.csv` are from the Figma Shop mock-up. They are **not verified merchant products**. Do not use generated jewellery images to represent real merchandise unless the item exactly matches.

For **each** product obtain:
1. Actual supplier item URL and SKU/variant mapping (size, metal colour and stone).
2. At least 3 authorised, true-to-product photos: white/neutral catalog, close-up detail, on-model.
3. Accurate composition, dimensions, care instructions and any required product compliance information.
4. Final GBP pricing after shipping, tax, payment, returns and target margin are accounted for.
5. Verified UK delivery and dispatch windows, refund and return policy.
6. Wix Stores product record created and tested via its product page and cart.

Only then adapt campaign imagery and paid advertising for the real item. Do not add unsupported product claims.
