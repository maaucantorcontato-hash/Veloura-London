## How to find other suppliers at zero subscription cost
1. In AliExpress, set **Ship to: United Kingdom** and **Currency: GBP**. Search product styles rather than store brands (e.g. minimalist gold-tone pendant; small hoop earrings; adjustable cuff bracelet; signet ring).
2. Compare **at least 3 different seller shops** for each promising style, not just different items from the same shop.
3. For each candidate, capture **exact item URL**, seller store URL, seller rating where available, order count shown, recent photo reviews, selected variant price, VAT where shown, shipping cost and delivery estimate to a UK postcode.
4. Ask shortlisted suppliers to confirm **dropshipping to the UK**, neutral packaging, sample order availability, material/compliance documentation and advertising rights for photos/videos.
5. Calculate landed cost in GBP (selected variant + shipping + duties/taxes that apply + expected returns + payment fees) before setting retail prices. A low advertised listing price is not guaranteed to be the final cost.
6. Compare final candidates by style fit, true quality, delivery reliability, total landed margin and evidence of demand. Order and inspect samples before investing in ads when feasible.
7. Only after approval, register real catalog products in the **Wix Studio 'My Site 2'** store and connect Figma product cards. Keep products hidden until fulfilment and accurate product content are confirmed.

## Product approval scorecard
Use a 0–5 scale for each item, with **quality / UK delivery / style / gross margin / trustworthy recent reviews**. No automatic 'winning product' claims; sales counts displayed on AliExpress are imperfect signals, not verified UK customer sales.

## Sourcing cautions
- AliExpress product prices and seller ratings change; screenshots are not live quotes.
- One listing may have many variant prices. Record the **chosen variant** specifically.
- An 'Orders' sort ranks according to the platform's displayed metric, not market demand in Britain.
- If one product comes from another supplier, each supplier may dispatch separately. Set realistic delivery expectations and returns responsibilities.
- Comply with UK consumer and product safety obligations; avoid unsupported 'hypoallergenic', '925 silver', 'solid gold', waterproof, or same-day delivery claims.
